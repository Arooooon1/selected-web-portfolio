- No rainbow lighting, stock gaming shields, testimonial inventions or unverified awards.

## Scene plan and asset roles

1. STRIKE 75 hero: generous titanium stage, oversized type, a hovering isolated keyboard at depth 3; restrained orbital guides at depth 2; UI at depth 4. Scroll transitions into the separated construction view.
2. Product orbit: eight flagship devices, draggable horizontal selection, depth and spotlight changes; selected item expands into the focus view with gallery and details.
3. Catalog: 24 distinct models, eight category filters, search and sorting, direct add-to-cart and comparison.
4. Switch lab: oversized key/switch interaction and user-triggered sound illustration, no autoplay sound.
5. Setup: original full-bleed desk composition at depth 0, interactive product hotspots and a coherent preset bundle.

Additional confirmed scope: real volumetric viewing of keyboard, mouse and headphones with animated lighting visible from multiple sides. LIGHT LAB therefore uses three original procedural Three.js concept meshes, free orbit and zoom, five lighting modes, color/brightness/speed controls, night lighting, per-key reaction and a shared SYNC scene. Flat renders remain the photographic catalog assets. The 3D concepts are not claimed to be factory CAD geometry.

All isolated renders use actual alpha transparency and shape-following shadows. Brand proposal boards and the setup photograph retain their intentional backgrounds. Large imagery is encoded locally in WebP, with smaller catalog variants.

## Functional boundaries

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, consistent with the existing portfolio. A local build in savage-source/ creates the single-file HTML entry point with inline CSS and JavaScript plus local assets. LocalStorage stores language, cart, comparison and motion preference. No server, account, real payment or order delivery is implied. Checkout clearly identifies its demo behavior.

## Motion

Native scroll stays available. Transform/opacity/clip-path animation, viewport-aware idle effects, reduced-motion support and lighter touch-device effects. Keyboard and touchscreen access accompany dragging. Model/brand names never enter the translation dictionary.
